SINGING

Welcome to Show Mode

The queue becomes a rotation of singers, each with up to 5 songs. The turn moves to the next singer on its own.

1Add music as usual — you'll be asked who it's for. Type a new name to create the singer.
2Singers join the rotation in the order they're added. Drag to reorder.
3Hit play. Use Change song for last-minute requests and Manage singers for everything else.

End the show?

You'll see the night's report and can export it before everything is cleared.

PLAYER

PlayKaraoke Player is a free, browser-based karaoke player built for real karaoke nights — CDG and MP4 files, key change, a second screen for the singers and a singer rotation for hosts.

The story

PlayKaraoke was created by Jorge Domecildes, a Brazilian karaoke enthusiast. The brand started in 2015 with the karaoke tracks he produced himself, and grew into PlayKaraoke Player: a tool made by someone who runs karaoke nights, for everyone who loves them.

PlayKaraoke Player is free to use. If it makes your nights better, you can support the project.
